# Fraud Engineering Studio

The Fraud Engineering Studio finds fraud that targets your institution and your customers — fake pages and ads that
impersonate your brand, look-alike websites, scam offers, accounts that receive stolen money — and takes it from the
first signal to a decided case.

Every detection is written in plain language, built from named conditions, tested on your data and versioned. An
analyst, a fraud engineer and an auditor read the same thing.
